04 / Principles

How the identity behaves.

Show the workflow

Use traces, contours, tool states, real spatial artifacts, and intermediate results. Avoid generic AI imagery.

Be precise

Describe what the agent does, the data it touches, and where the limitations are. Evidence beats superlatives.

Let the system breathe

Use large type, strong alignment, broad paper surfaces, and restrained motion. Not every element needs a container.

Do not perform futurism

No glowing brains, neon gradients, magic language, or fake dashboards. The actual engineering is interesting enough.
