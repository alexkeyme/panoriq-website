How to read your result - total / 24

These bands are a discussion guide, not a validated benchmark or release gate. Review each dimension: a high total can hide a critical gap.

0-8
Build the foundations. Start with explicit success criteria, a small set of representative cases, and enough trace visibility to investigate failures.
9-16
Connect the pieces. Review your lowest-scoring dimensions. Link evaluation runs, failure investigation, and production feedback into a regular process.
17-24
Test the depth. Challenge dataset coverage, grader quality, and consistency across runs. A strong self-assessment score does not establish production reliability.
Start with expectations

Define what success looks like before you build, and make those expectations something you can repeatedly test. Start with a few real tasks and clear criteria. Grow the checks as you learn from users and failures.
